Delete Your CostMate Account

App: CostMate (io.obura.costmate), published by Obura

Delete your account in the app (recommended)

  1. Open CostMate and sign in.
  2. Go to Settings (bottom-right tab).
  3. Scroll to the Account section and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. Your account is deleted immediately.

Or request deletion by email

If you can no longer sign in, email comporell@gmail.com from the email address associated with your CostMate account, with the subject "CostMate account deletion request". We will delete the account and confirm within 30 days.

What gets deleted

Deletion is permanent and removes all data associated with the account: your sign-in credentials (email/password and any linked Google identity), your business, materials, products, recipes, price history, equipment, expenses, and uploaded photos. No data is retained after deletion.

See also the CostMate privacy policy.
